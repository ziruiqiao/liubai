# Weather data

Weather data by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/).
Data are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The hosted free API is used for this application's personal, noncommercial use.

The app converts WMO condition codes into stylized clouds, wind, and mild or
heavy rain and snow animation. These artistic effects are not weather observations.
There is no thunder sound, flashing lightning, or automatic sky darkening.

The system location is rounded to 0.1 degrees before weather requests or
reverse geocoding. Coordinates and location history are not stored in app data.
